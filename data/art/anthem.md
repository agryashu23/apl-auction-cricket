# APL anthem: "Raise the Paddle"

Original English lyrics for the Auction Premier League. No real league, team,
sponsor or player names.

## Style prompt (paste into Suno / Udio "style" box)

```
stadium anthem, high-energy cricket theme song, big drums and brass stabs,
electronic beat, crowd chants, confident male lead vocal with a gang chorus,
English lyrics, 124 BPM, triumphant, cinematic build, radio-ready mix
```

## Getting it into the game

1. Generate on a **paid** plan of the music tool. Free plans don't allow commercial
   use, and the game is going on the Play Store.
2. Get a full version (≈2 min). An **instrumental** version is useful too, for a
   menu loop.
3. Save it as `anthem.mp3` (or `.ogg`) and send it over. I'll trim it to a clean
   loop and drop it in `client-src/Assets/Resources/Music/`. The game already plays
   it on the title screen (fading out when the auction starts) and on the results
   screen.
